Common questions

Questions about working with an NDIS consultant.

What does an NDIS consultant do?

It depends on who you ask. Some consultants handle registration paperwork. Some prepare you for audits. Others do marketing. We focus on policies, social media and websites, so your paperwork and your online page both show the care you already give.

Do you help with NDIS registration?

Not directly yet. We don't lodge registration or act on your behalf, and no one can promise a registration result. We write the policies and evidence system that registration and audits rely on, and more registration support is coming soon.

How much does an NDIS consultant cost?

Our prices include GST and are on the website: the Gap Check is $166, policies start from $1,755, social media from $1,423 a month and websites from $1,380. You get a fixed written quote before any paid work starts. See all prices.

Do you work with providers outside Melbourne?

Yes. We're in Melbourne and work with NDIS providers across Australia, online.
