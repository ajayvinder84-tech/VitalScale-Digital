Common questions

Questions about the free week

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, and nothing to pay, now or later. It's one free week of posts per NDIS business. We do this because it's easier to show you our work than to tell you about it. If you like it, you can ask about more. If not, no hard feelings.

What do I need to send?

Just the short form on this page: your business name, the services you offer, the area you cover and how people refer to you. Your logo, brand colours and Instagram or Facebook link help, but they're optional. Nothing about participants, please.

How long does it take?

Once we have your details, we send your week of posts to you within 5 working days. The form is short, and the optional parts can stay blank.

Do I have to buy anything after?

No. There's no obligation and no lock-in. If you'd like monthly help later on, just ask. We only add you to our email list if you tick the box.

Do you need participant information?

No, never. Your posts are about your business: your services, your area and how to refer. Please don't send participant names, photos or stories.

Can I use the posts if I don't work with you?

Yes. We send the posts to you, and you upload them yourself. You don't have to buy anything or work with us after.
