Why "safely" matters

Your staff may already be using AI. The question is how.

Support workers paste shift notes into free chatbots to tidy them up. It's quick, but those notes can hold private participant information. Disability-specific guidance on AI is still catching up, so providers need their own clear rules.

"Personal information is not input into publicly available generative AI tools as a matter of best practice."

Australian privacy regulator (OAIC) AI guidance, as summarised by Clayton Utz

"Shift notes potentially include personal (and private) information about the participant."

Confidentiality

How we keep participant information private.

The simplest protection: VitalScale never holds your participants' information. Everything is set up inside your own accounts, and we build and test it with made-up data.

  • We check every tool before you use itWhere data is stored, whether it trains on your data, how long it keeps it, who can access it, and whether you can switch it off.
  • Often it's software you already haveMany NDIS systems now include AI features. We help you switch on only what's safe and switch off the rest.
  • Least information inStaff use initials or an ID, not full names. We don't rely on this alone, because one person's note can still identify them.
  • Participants are toldWe update your privacy notice and consent forms so participants know how AI is used and can say no.
  • A written AI-use policy and tool registerWhich tools are approved, for what, and what must never be entered.
  • Mistakes get reportedWrong AI output or information put in the wrong tool is logged and handled through your incident process.

We sign a confidentiality agreement with every client.

How we work

Workflow first, tools second.

We don't automate anything until we understand how your week actually runs.

  1. Map your admin

    Find the tasks your team repeats every shift and every week.

  2. Pick one or two

    Choose the safest, highest-value uses. Sometimes the answer isn't AI.

  3. Set up and train

    Approved tools, a written AI-use policy and a short team training session.

  4. Check after 30 days

    Is it saving time? Is the record quality holding up? Adjust or stop.
