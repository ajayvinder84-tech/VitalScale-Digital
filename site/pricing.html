Common questions

Questions about prices.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Every price includes GST, and you get a fixed written quote before any work starts. Nothing is added without your written OK.

Do your prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on this site includes GST. "From" means the starting price, and your quote shows the exact total.

What happens after my $166 check?

We go through your report with you on a call, then give you 2–3 clear options in a fixed written quote. If you go ahead within 30 days, the full $166 comes off the price.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. The Website Care Plan ($166/month) covers hosting, updates and small edits, and Scroll-stopping Social Media continues every month with a shoot, 5 posts a week and a monthly check on enquiries and messages. Policies come with plain-English staff guides and a change log.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. Our monthly plans (Scroll-stopping Social Media and the Website Care Plan) have no lock-in. You can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

What report will I get?

The Gap Check gives you a written gap report with your top gaps ranked and a plain-English fix for each, plus a 30-minute call. The Trust Check gives you a 10–15 minute video walkthrough, a 1-page checklist of fixes in order, plus a 20-minute call. Social media clients get a short monthly report on enquiries, messages, calls and referrals, not likes.
