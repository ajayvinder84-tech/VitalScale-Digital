Priority 1

Incident reporting steps

Your policy says

"All incidents are identified, reported, recorded and managed in a timely manner."

What your team told us

Support workers text their team leader. Nobody is sure who fills in the incident form, or when.

Plain-English fix

Write the steps in order: who to tell, who fills in the form, by when, and where it's kept. Then walk your team through it.

Priority 2

Consent records

Your policy says

"Written consent is collected from each participant and kept on their file."

What your team told us

Staff often ask for consent out loud at intake. Some forms are on paper, some are in emails, and nobody is sure which are current.

Plain-English fix

Ask in a way each person understands, then record their consent in writing. Keep it in one place, and check it at each service review.

Priority 3

Complaints records

Your policy says

"All complaints are recorded in the complaints register and followed up."

What your team told us

Small complaints get sorted out on the spot, so they're never written down. The register has no entries this year.

Plain-English fix

Record every complaint, even small ones sorted on the day, with what you did about it. It shows you listen, and helps you spot patterns.
