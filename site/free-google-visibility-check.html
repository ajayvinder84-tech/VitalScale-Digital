Common questions

Questions about the free visibility check

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, and nothing to pay, now or later. It's one free check per NDIS business. If you want help with the fixes after, you get a fixed written quote first. If not, no hard feelings.

What do I need to send?

Just the short form on this page: your business name, your website, your main suburb and the services you offer. Nothing about participants, please.

How long does it take?

Once we have your details, you get your report by email within 5 working days.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

No one can promise that, and we don't. The report shows where you stand today and what to fix first. What happens after depends on your website, your area and how many other providers are nearby.

Where do the numbers come from?

Search figures are estimates from Semrush, a search data tool, and we show the date we checked. Treat them as a guide. Positions also change from day to day and from one location to the next.

Do you need access to my website or Google account?

No. We only look at what anyone can see on Google. Please don't send passwords or logins.

Do I have to buy anything after?

No. There's no obligation and no lock-in. We only add you to our email list if you tick the box.
