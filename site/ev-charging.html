What I do

Site checks and cleaning

Scheduled visits to check each charger works, the cable and plug are clean and undamaged, signs are clear and the bay is usable. You get a short photo report after every visit.

Fault reporting and repair coordination

When a charger shows an error, I try a remote reset through the charger software first. If it needs a repair, I raise it with the supplier or installer under warranty and follow it up until it's fixed.

New chargers for businesses

For wineries, motels and cafes: choosing the right charger, getting quotes from licensed electricians, grants, and the app listings so drivers can find you.

Charging spots people remember

Signs, bay marking, seating and a photo-worthy spot, so a charging stop turns into a tasting, a meal or a night's stay.

Why it matters

A broken charger costs more than a repair. A driver who arrives to a dead charger leaves a bad review on PlugShare or Google, and the next driver goes somewhere else. Public chargers in Australia have a reliability problem: an Arcadis review of PlugShare data looked at 5,794 public charger sites and found maintenance problems are common.

Most charger owners don't have anyone whose job it is to look at the charger. That's the gap I fill.

Common questions

What does EV charger maintenance include?

Checking each charger turns on and charges, inspecting and cleaning the cable, plug and screen, checking signs and bay access, trying remote resets for errors, and reporting anything that needs a licensed electrician or the supplier.

Do you do electrical work?

No. All electrical work is done by licensed electricians or the charger supplier. I coordinate them, follow up, and make sure the job gets finished.

Who pays for repairs?

Faults in the first years are usually covered by the charger warranty through the supplier or installer. Damage, like a car hitting a charger, usually goes through the owner's insurance. I help with both.

Can you help a council or business that already has chargers?

Yes. Existing sites are the main focus: regular checks, quick fault reporting and a record of every visit.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many chargers and how far away the site is. You get a fixed written quote before any work starts.
