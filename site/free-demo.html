Common questions

Questions about the free demo

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, and nothing to pay, now or later. We design one homepage for your business so you can see how we work before you spend anything. If you want the full site after, you get a fixed written quote first. If not, no hard feelings.

What do I need to send?

Just the short form on this page: your business name, the services you offer, the area you cover and how people refer to you. Your current website, logo, brand colours and Instagram or Facebook link help, but they're optional. Nothing about participants, please.

How long does it take?

Once we have your details, you get your demo [TURNAROUND]. The form is short, and the optional parts can stay blank.

Is the demo a full website?

No. It's one homepage, shown on a computer and a phone, to give you a feel for what your website could be like. A full website has more pages. It's a separate job, from $1,380 inc GST, and only if you want it.

Do I have to buy anything after?

No. There's no obligation and no lock-in. If you'd like the full website later, you get a fixed written quote before any work starts, and nothing is added without your written OK. We only add you to our email list if you tick the box.

Do you need participant information?

No, never. A homepage is about your business: your services, your area and how to refer. Please don't send participant names, photos or stories.
