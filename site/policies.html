The real problem

Audits check what you do, not just what you wrote.

A template can look complete and still fail. The most common gap is a policy that says one thing while the team does another. Auditors look for proof that each policy is actually happening, in five places:

  • RecordsParticipant files, service agreements and case notes that match the policy.
  • ConsentSigned, current and stored where you can find it.
  • IncidentsA register that's filled in and followed up.
  • ComplaintsLogged, answered and learned from.
  • WorkforceScreening, training and staff who can explain the process.

4.2 Incident Reporting

The Organisation shall ensure that all incidents are identified, reported, recorded and managed in accordance with relevant legislation, regulations and the NDIS Practice Standards in a timely manner. All Workers are required to comply with this Policy. Appropriate action shall be taken as necessary.

Who reports? To whom? By when? Where is it recorded? The template doesn't say.

After · Harbourlight Supportsv2.0 · plain English
Straight away
Make sure the participant and everyone else is safe. Call 000 if anyone is hurt or in danger.
Same shift
Phone your team leader (roster has the on-call number), then fill in the incident form in ShiftCare before you finish.
Within 24 hrs
The team leader decides if it's a reportable incident. If so, the director notifies the NDIS Commission within the required timeframe.
Proof at audit
Incident register entry, the notification record, and staff training sign-off for this policy.
