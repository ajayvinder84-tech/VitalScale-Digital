Common questions

Questions providers ask us.

How do I get my first NDIS clients?

Most first clients come through people: support coordinators, local community groups and word of mouth. Make it easy for them to check you out, with a clear website, a Google Business Profile, a current social media page and one simple way to refer. Then keep showing up, because referrers remember the providers who stay visible.

Is a consultant worth it, or can I do it myself?

You can do a lot yourself, and we'll tell you honestly if you don't need us. A consultant is worth it when they save you time and stress you can't spare. Be wary of anyone who guarantees registration or audit results, because nobody honestly can.
