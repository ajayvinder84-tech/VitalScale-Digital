Should Your Business Install an EV Charger? Costs, Payback and Grants in Victoria (2026)

By Ajay Singh, VitalScale Digital

Quick answer: For most regional businesses, an EV charger pays for itself through extra customers, not through the electricity it sells. A 22 kW AC charger installed usually costs around $4,000 to $12,000 all up, depending on your power supply. If it brings in a few extra visitors a week who stay to eat, taste or sleep, payback is often around two years.

Who an EV charger makes sense for

Slow (AC) chargers suit places where people already stay for an hour or more: wineries and cellar doors, motels and B&Bs, cafes and restaurants on touring routes, and tourist attractions. Drivers plan trips around chargers, so a charger puts you on the map inside apps like PlugShare and Google Maps.

Fast (DC) chargers cost $40,000 or more and suit highway sites where people stop for 20 minutes. Most small businesses don't need one.

Prices change, so always get two written quotes. A 22 kW charger only charges at 22 kW if your site has three-phase power and the car can take it. Many cars max out at 7 or 11 kW on AC.

What it earns

At around 45 to 60 cents per kWh, a typical session makes a profit of only a few dollars on electricity. The real return is the visit. A couple who charge for 90 minutes at a winery might spend $50 to $150 on tastings, lunch and a few bottles.

Grants to watch

Grant rounds open and close through the year. In Victoria, past rounds of tourism and business energy grants have helped wineries and cellar doors with charger costs, and some councils offer environmental upgrade finance. Check Business Victoria, your local council and Regional Development Victoria before you buy, because most grants won't pay for work you've already started.

How to avoid a charger that loses money

  • Choose a charger with an Australian office or supplier and at least a 2-year warranty.
  • Make sure it supports OCPP, the common software standard, so you can change apps later and reset faults remotely.
  • List it on PlugShare, Google Maps and your charging app on day one.
  • Have someone check it regularly. A broken charger with bad reviews is worse than no charger.

Common questions

How much does it cost to install an EV charger for a business in Victoria?

Usually around $4,000 to $12,000 for one 22 kW AC charger, installed and set up. A three-phase power upgrade can add $2,500 to $10,000 or more.

Is an EV charger worth it for a winery or motel?

Often yes, because charging takes 1 to 3 hours and drivers spend that time on your food, wine or rooms. The electricity margin alone is small.

Can I get a grant for an EV charger?

Sometimes. Victorian tourism, business energy and council programs have funded chargers in past rounds. Check what's open before you buy.
