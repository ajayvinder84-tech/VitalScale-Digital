## 1. Verdict

**FIX FIRST — 6 wording fixes and 1 missing point.** Every date in the script checks out. That includes 20 November: it's the date in the Act. One Department timeline says 27 November, but the law wins.

**Most important fix:** say the ban covers gifts to *anyone*, not just participants. The Act says "it does not matter whether the person is the participant". The Government's own explanation says a kickback includes a payment "in exchange for … a business referral". Paying support coordinators for referrals sits right in that zone.

## 4. Missing Steps

Five steps a viewer needs that the script skips. The first one carries the most risk.

1. **Check who else you give things to** (§4, after "Not allowed"). The ban covers gifts to *anyone* reasonably likely to get you engaged: family members, support coordinators, anyone. The Act says "it does not matter whether the person is the participant". The Government says a kickback includes a payment "in exchange for … a business referral". So review any referral fees or "thank you" gifts, not just ads. [Act s 73VA(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00066/asmade/2026-08-20/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) · [Health fact sheet](https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-08/final-changes-to-the-ndis-amendment-securing-the-ndis-for-future-generations-bill-2026.pdf)
2. **Know which records to keep** (§3, after the 7-year line). The NDIA lists invoices, support logs, rosters, case notes and service agreements. Invoices alone won't prove the support happened. [NDIA record keeping](https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/working-provider/reporting-and-recording-keeping/what-are-record-keeping-requirements)
3. **Owners: check you can get a Strong myID** (§3, after the PRODA line). Linking your business in RAM needs a Strong myID. Strong needs an Australian passport (or a WA driver's licence). Owners without one should start early and ask the Commission about other options. [Commission guide](https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/portal-quick-reference-guides/changing-proda-myid-and-ram) · [myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/help/verifying-your-id)
4. **Get agreement before charging the new price** (§4, after the price line). The NDIA says providers "must discuss any proposed changes to existing service agreements" and "participants must agree" first. Bill the old price for supports delivered before 1 December. [NDIA news, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.ndis.gov.au/news/11733-ndis-price-increase-1-december-2026-gender-based-undervaluation-priority-awards-review)
5. **Talk to your plan managers now** (§4, after the plan manager line). Their processing time comes out of your 90 days, because the clock starts when you deliver the support. Ask how fast they submit. [NDIS Act s 45A(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2013A00020/latest/text)
